PIXEL POMO ART KIT — HOW TO RUN (Windows)
==========================================

1) Double-click PixelPomoArtKit.exe. That is all.
   No install, no Python, no internet.

2) The first time, Windows shows a blue warning:
   "Windows protected your PC".
   That is normal — the file is unsigned. Click:
       "More info"  ->  "Run anyway"

3) YOUR DRAWINGS ARE NOT NEXT TO THE .EXE. They live in your user folder:
       C:\Users\<you>\AppData\Local\PixelPomoArtKit\
           library\       ->  your drawings. DO NOT DELETE.
           exports\       ->  sprites you export
           backups\       ->  a zip of the library before each update
           settings.json  ->  your favourite colours and last tool
   HELP -> "OPEN FOLDER" takes you straight there.
   So you can move the .exe anywhere, download a new one, or delete it —
   the drawings stay. (Older versions kept a library\ folder next to the
   .exe; the first start of this version copies those drawings over and
   tells you. The old folder is left as it was.)

4) Back up the library\ folder now and then (copy-paste is enough).
   The app also keeps a .bak of how each file looked when you opened it.

5) SAVING: every stroke is saved the moment you finish it. The SAVE button
   (or Ctrl+S) saves again and shows "saved HH:MM:SS" beside it. If you
   ever see "SAVE FAILED" there, tell the developer — it means the app
   cannot write to its folder.

6) UPDATES: the UPDATE button (bottom right, next to HELP) checks GitHub.
   When a new version exists the button says "UPDATE * vX.Y.Z". Click it:
   the app downloads the new version, zips your library into backups\,
   closes, replaces itself and reopens. Your drawings are never touched.
   The previous version is kept next to the new one as
   PixelPomoArtKit.old.exe — rename it back if you ever want it.


CONTROLS
--------
  Left click       draw (one click, one square)
  Right click      eyedropper — picks up the colour under the cursor
  Drag             continuous line
  b / e / f        brush / eraser / fill bucket (paints the connected area)
  m                symmetry: OFF -> MIRROR -> STICK
  Ctrl+S           save                Ctrl+N   new drawing
  Ctrl+Z           undo                Ctrl+Y   redo
  Mouse wheel, + - zoom in / out
  F1               help panel (every button and key)   Esc closes it
  Colours          panel on the right:
                   top strip  = hue
                   square below = light / dark
                   click or drag either one
  The ink          the #rrggbb box above the colours. Click it and type a
                   code, Enter applies. Double-click selects it to copy.
                   "+" beside it adds the colour to your favourites.
                   Right-click a favourite to remove it.
  SYMMETRY         under the colours. Three modes:
                   OFF     normal painting.
                   MIRROR  click the canvas where the bar should stand.
                           "| 90" = standing bar (mirrors left/right),
                           "- 180" = lying bar (mirrors top/bottom).
                           "length" = how many squares long. Painting along
                           the bar is mirrored across it. To MOVE the bar:
                           press on it and drag, or PLACE BAR and click.
                   STICK   every click paints "length" squares in one go —
                           to the right (- 180) or downward (| 90).


THE THREE PANES
---------------
  Left    your drawings. Click one to open it.
          "+ NEW DRAWING" asks for a size: Flower, Bush, Rock, Tree, or your own.
          "IMPORT PNG..." brings in art from another program and saves it
          into your library right away.
          The  menu on each row: Duplicate, Export, Rename, Species, Size, Delete.
  Middle  the canvas.
  Right   SAVE, tools, colours, symmetry; and in the bottom-right corner
          two live previews — one at real size, one at a "squint test"
          distance — the drawing's size (click it to resize), UPDATE, HELP.


SENDING YOUR WORK BACK
----------------------
  Best:   send the .json file from the library\ folder.
          That IS the drawing — nothing is lost.
  Or:      menu -> "Export PNG..." and send the image.

  Palettes, letter grids, engine settings — none of that is your job.
  You draw and send it; the developer converts it.


IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
------------------------
  Every drawing is a plain text file in library\. It is safe to open, copy,
  or send. Nothing you do in this app can damage the game.
