PIXEL POMO ART KIT — HOW TO RUN (Windows)
==========================================

1) Double-click PixelPomoArtKit.exe. That is all.
   No install, no Python, no internet.

2) The first time, Windows shows a blue warning:
   "Windows protected your PC".
   That is normal — the file is unsigned. Click:
       "More info"  ->  "Run anyway"

3) The app creates two folders next to itself:
       library\   ->  your drawings live here. DO NOT DELETE.
       exports\   ->  sprites you export land here

4) Back up the library\ folder now and then (copy-paste is enough).
   The app also keeps a .bak of how each file looked when you opened it.


CONTROLS
--------
  Left click       draw (one click, one square)
  Right click      eyedropper — picks up the colour under the cursor
  Drag             continuous line
  f                fill bucket (paints the connected area)
  e                eraser              b   brush
  Ctrl+Z           undo                Ctrl+Y   redo
  Mouse wheel      zoom in / out
  Colours          panel at the bottom right:
                   top strip  = hue
                   square below = light / dark
                   click or drag either one


THE THREE PANES
---------------
  Left    your drawings. Click one to open it.
          "+ NEW DRAWING" starts a blank one.
          The  menu on each row: Duplicate, Export, Rename, Species, Size, Delete.
  Middle  the canvas, plus two live previews — one at real size, one at a
          "squint test" distance, both drawn through the game's own code.
  Right   tools and colours.


SENDING YOUR WORK BACK
----------------------
  Best:   send the .json file from the library\ folder.
          That IS the drawing — nothing is lost.
  Or:      menu -> "Export PNG..." and send the image.

  Palettes, letter grids, engine settings — none of that is your job.
  You draw and send it; the developer converts it.


IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
------------------------
  Every drawing is a plain text file in library\. It is safe to open, copy,
  or send. Nothing you do in this app can damage the game.
