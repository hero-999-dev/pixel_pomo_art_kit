PIXEL POMO ART KIT — macOS SETUP (step by step)
================================================

STEP 0 — DOWNLOAD THE RIGHT ONE
-------------------------------
There are TWO Mac downloads. Picking the wrong one gives you an app that
will not open, with an unhelpful error. Check which Mac you have:

    Apple menu (top left)  ->  "About This Mac"

    "Chip: Apple M1 / M2 / M3 / M4..."  ->  PixelPomoArtKit-macos-apple-silicon.zip
    "Processor: Intel Core..."          ->  PixelPomoArtKit-macos-intel.zip

If you are not sure, Apple Silicon is the right guess for any Mac bought
from late 2020 onwards.


STEP 1 — UNZIP
--------------
Double-click the .zip you downloaded.
You get PixelPomoArtKit.app.


STEP 2 — MOVE THE APP  (do not skip this)
-----------------------------------------
Drag PixelPomoArtKit.app from Downloads into your Applications folder.

Why: macOS runs unsigned apps launched from Downloads out of a randomised,
read-only copy of themselves. The app misbehaves from there. Moving it out
of Downloads stops that.


STEP 3 — FIRST LAUNCH
---------------------
The app is unsigned, so macOS blocks it the first time. This is expected and
does not mean anything is wrong with it.

Which method you need depends on your macOS version. Check it first:
    Apple menu -> "About This Mac" -> the version number

  A) macOS 15 (Sequoia), macOS 26 (TAHOE) AND NEWER   <-- most people
     Apple removed the old right-click shortcut in these versions, so
     right-clicking will NOT help. Do this instead:

     1. Double-click PixelPomoArtKit.app once.
     2. A warning appears saying it cannot be opened. Click "Done".
     3. Apple menu -> "System Settings"
     4. Click "Privacy & Security" in the sidebar
     5. Scroll DOWN to the "Security" section
     6. You will see a line like:
            "PixelPomoArtKit.app was blocked to protect your Mac"
        Click the "Open Anyway" button next to it.
     7. Confirm with your password or Touch ID.
     8. One final warning appears -> click "Open".

  B) macOS 14 (Sonoma) AND OLDER
     1. RIGHT-CLICK PixelPomoArtKit.app (or hold Control and click it)
     2. Choose "Open" from the menu
     3. In the warning, click "Open" again
     Note: plain double-clicking does NOT offer this choice. You have to
     right-click the first time.

  C) IF NEITHER WORKS — TERMINAL   (last resort, works on every version)
     1. Spotlight (Cmd + Space), type "Terminal", press Enter
     2. Paste this line and press Enter:

         xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/PixelPomoArtKit.app

     3. Then open the app normally by double-clicking.

You only do this on the FIRST launch. After that, double-click as usual.


STEP 4 — WHERE YOUR DRAWINGS LIVE
---------------------------------
On first launch the app creates:

    Documents / PixelPomoArtKit /
        library/   ->  your drawings. DO NOT DELETE.
        exports/   ->  sprites you export

In Documents, NOT next to the app. That is deliberate: when a new version
arrives and you drag it over the old app, your drawings are untouched.

Back up the library folder now and then — copying it somewhere is enough.


STEP 5 — IF macOS ASKS ABOUT "DOCUMENTS", CLICK ALLOW
-----------------------------------------------------
The first time the app saves, macOS may ask whether PixelPomoArtKit may
access your Documents folder. Click ALLOW.

If it was refused, the app tells you on launch and keeps your drawings in
    Library / Application Support / PixelPomoArtKit /
instead — nothing is lost. To move back to Documents:
    System Settings -> Privacy & Security -> Files and Folders
    -> PixelPomoArtKit -> turn on "Documents Folder", then restart the app.

SAVING: every stroke is saved the moment you finish it. The SAVE button
(or Cmd+S) saves again and shows "saved HH:MM:SS" beside it. If you ever
see "SAVE FAILED" there, tell the developer — it means the app cannot
write to its folder.


CONTROLS
--------
  Left click       draw (one click, one square)
  Right click      eyedropper — picks up the colour under the cursor
                   (two-finger tap, or hold Control and click)
  Drag             continuous line
  b / e / f        brush / eraser / fill bucket (paints the connected area)
  m                symmetry on / off
  Cmd+S            save                Cmd+N   new drawing
  Cmd+Z            undo                Cmd+Shift+Z or Cmd+Y   redo
                   (Ctrl works too, if that is what your hands know)
  Two-finger scroll / + -   zoom in and out
  F1               help panel (every button and key)   Esc closes it
  Colours          panel on the right:
                   top strip = hue, square below = light / dark
  The ink          the #rrggbb box above the colours. Click it and type a
                   code, Enter applies. Double-click selects it to copy.
                   "+" beside it adds the colour to your favourites.
                   Right-click a favourite to remove it.
  SYMMETRY         turn it on, then click the canvas where the bar should
                   stand. "| 90" = standing bar (mirrors left/right),
                   "- 180" = lying bar (mirrors top/bottom). The number is
                   how many squares long it is. Painting along the bar is
                   mirrored across it.


THE THREE PANES
---------------
  Left    your drawings. Click one to open it.
          "+ NEW DRAWING" asks for a size: Flower, Bush, Rock, Tree, or your own.
          The  menu on each row: Duplicate, Export, Rename, Species, Size, Delete.
  Middle  the canvas.
  Right   SAVE, tools, colours; and in the bottom-right corner two live
          previews — one at real size, one at a "squint test" distance —
          the drawing's size (click it to resize), and HELP.


SENDING YOUR WORK BACK
----------------------
  Best:   send the .json file from Documents/PixelPomoArtKit/library/.
          That IS the drawing — nothing is lost.
  Or:      menu -> "Export PNG..." and send the image.

  Palettes, letter grids, engine settings — none of that is your job.
  You draw and send it; the developer converts it.
