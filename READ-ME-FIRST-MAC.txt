PIXEL POMO ART KIT — macOS SETUP (step by step)
================================================

CHECK THIS FIRST
----------------
This build runs on APPLE SILICON only (M1, M2, M3, M4...).
It will NOT open on an Intel Mac.

To check:  Apple menu (top left)  ->  "About This Mac"
    "Chip: Apple M..."      ->  good, carry on
    "Processor: Intel..."   ->  this file will not run for you.
                                Ask the developer for an Intel build —
                                it can be added to the same download page.


STEP 1 — UNZIP
--------------
Double-click PixelPomoArtKit-macos.zip.
You get PixelPomoArtKit.app.


STEP 2 — MOVE THE APP  (do not skip this)
-----------------------------------------
Drag PixelPomoArtKit.app from Downloads into your Applications folder.

Why: macOS runs unsigned apps launched from Downloads out of a randomised,
read-only copy of themselves. The app misbehaves from there. Moving it out
of Downloads stops that.


STEP 3 — FIRST LAUNCH
---------------------
The app is unsigned, so macOS blocks it the first time. This is expected.
Two ways — try A first:

  A) RIGHT-CLICK METHOD   (macOS 14 and earlier)
     - RIGHT-CLICK PixelPomoArtKit.app (or Control-click it)
     - Choose "Open" from the menu
     - In the warning that appears, click "Open" again
     IMPORTANT: double-clicking does NOT offer this choice.
     You must right-click the first time.

  B) SETTINGS METHOD   (macOS 15 / Sequoia and later — if A has no "Open")
     - Double-click the app once, then dismiss the warning
     - Apple menu -> "System Settings"
     - "Privacy & Security" in the sidebar
     - Scroll DOWN to the "Security" section
     - You will see a line like:
         "PixelPomoArtKit.app was blocked to protect your Mac"
     - Click "Open Anyway"
     - Confirm with your password or Touch ID
     - One more warning appears -> click "Open"

  C) IF NEITHER WORKS — TERMINAL   (last resort)
     - Spotlight (Cmd + Space), type "Terminal", press Enter
     - Paste this and press Enter:

         xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/PixelPomoArtKit.app

     - Then open the app normally by double-clicking.

You only do this on the FIRST launch. After that, double-click as usual.


STEP 4 — WHERE YOUR DRAWINGS LIVE
---------------------------------
On first launch the app creates:

    Documents / PixelPomoArtKit /
        library/   ->  your drawings. DO NOT DELETE.
        exports/   ->  sprites you export

In Documents, NOT next to the app. That is deliberate: when a new version
arrives and you drag it over the old app, your drawings are untouched.

Back up the library folder now and then — copying it somewhere is enough.


CONTROLS
--------
  Left click       draw (one click, one square)
  Right click      eyedropper — picks up the colour under the cursor
  Drag             continuous line
  f                fill bucket        e   eraser        b   brush
  Cmd+Z            undo               Cmd+Shift+Z   redo
  Two-finger scroll / + -   zoom in and out
  Colours          panel at the bottom right:
                   top strip = hue, square below = light / dark


THE THREE PANES
---------------
  Left    your drawings. Click one to open it.
          "+ NEW DRAWING" starts a blank one.
          The  menu on each row: Duplicate, Export, Rename, Species, Size, Delete.
  Middle  the canvas, plus two live previews — one at real size, one at a
          "squint test" distance, both drawn through the game's own code.
  Right   tools and colours.


SENDING YOUR WORK BACK
----------------------
  Best:   send the .json file from Documents/PixelPomoArtKit/library/.
          That IS the drawing — nothing is lost.
  Or:      menu -> "Export PNG..." and send the image.

  Palettes, letter grids, engine settings — none of that is your job.
  You draw and send it; the developer converts it.
